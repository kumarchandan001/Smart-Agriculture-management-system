🎯 CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NITROGEN (N) - mg/kg
  Low (<50):      Affects all crops, stunted growth
  Medium (50-100): Adequate for most crops
  High (>100):    Excess, risk of disease

PHOSPHORUS (P) - mg/kg
  Low (<20):      Poor root development
  Medium (20-40):  Good for flowering & root growth
  High (>40):     May lock other nutrients

POTASSIUM (K) - mg/kg
  Low (<100):     Weak disease resistance
  Medium (100-200): Good immunity & stress tolerance
  High (>200):    Excellent crop quality

pH SCALE
  Acidic (<6):    Nutrient solubility issues
  Neutral (6-7.5): Ideal for most crops
  Alkaline (>7.5): Iron deficiency risk

HEALTH SCORE (0-100)
  Excellent (80+):   All nutrients well-balanced
  Good (60-80):      Minor deficiencies
  Fair (40-60):      Moderate corrections needed
  Poor (<40):        Major interventions required
